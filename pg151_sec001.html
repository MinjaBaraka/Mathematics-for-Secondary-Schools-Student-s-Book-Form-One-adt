Example 6.23

Find the gradient and y-intercept in each of the following lines:

(a)\; 12x + 4y - 15 = 0
(b)\; 24 - 8x - 3y = 0

Solution

(a)Given 12x+4y−15=0.

Make y the subject of the formula. Thus,

4y = -12x + 15

Dividing by 4 both sides of the given equation gives,

y=−3x+154

Compare this equation with y=mx+c gives m=−3 and c=154.

(0,154) is the y-intercept.

Therefore, the gradient is −3 and the y-intercept is (0,154).

(b)Given 24−8x−3y=0.

Make y the subject of the formula:

-3y = 8x - 24

Dividing by −3 on both sides gives,

y=−83x+8

Compare this equation with y=mx+c to get m=−83 and c=8.

Therefore, the gradient is −83 and the y-intercept is (0,8).
