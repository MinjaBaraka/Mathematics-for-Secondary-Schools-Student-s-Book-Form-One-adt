General equation of a straight line

Equation in the form y=mx+c is sometimes referred to as slope-intercept equation. Equation of straight line can also be expressed in a general form. Referring equations (1) and (2) which have same gradient, that is:

y−y1x−x1=y2−y1x2−x1(4)

Equation (4) can be rearranged to get,

(y2−y1)x−(x2−x1)y+y1x2−x1y2=0

Since x1, y1, x2, and y2 are given real numbers, then (y2−y1), −(x2−x1), and y1x2−x1y2 are also real numbers. Thus, they can be replaced by constants a, b, and c, respectively. Therefore, the equation can be reduced to

ax+by+c=0(5)

Equation (5) is called the general equation of a straight line.
