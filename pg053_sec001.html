Example 3.11

Round off the following numbers.

  • (i) 1.88500 to two decimal places.
  • (ii) 2.682450 to four decimal places.

Solution

  • (i) 1.88500 rounded off to two decimal places is 1.88 since the digits after 5 are all zero.
  • (ii) 2.682450 rounded off to four decimal places is 2.6824 as the digit after 5 is 0.

Example 3.12

  • (i) Round off 8.6521 to one decimal place.
  • (ii) Round off 81.26584 to two decimal places.

Solution

  • (i) 8.6521 is rounded off as 8.7 to one decimal place.
