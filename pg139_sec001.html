Example 6.10

Find the equation of a straight line which passes through the points A (3, 7) and B (–2, –3).

Solution

Using the formula for finding an equation of a straight line,

y−y1x−x1=y2−y1x2−x1

Let (x1,y1)=(3,7) and (x2,y2)=(−2,−3). Substitute these values into the equation as follows:

y−7x−3=−3−7−2−3

Simplification gives,

y−7x−3=−10−5
y−7x−3=2

Cross multiply and rearrange to get, y=2x+1.

Therefore, the equation of the line passing through the given points is y=2x+1.
