Hence, x=23.

Therefore, the larger number is 23 and the smaller number is 7.

Example 5.39

Dashi has 83,000 Tanzanian shillings for shopping. If he buys 2 ties and 2 shirts, he remains with 9,000 Tanzanian shillings. If he buys 1 tie and 3 shirts he spends all the money. Find the price of a tie and a shirt.

Solution

Let: p be the price of a tie in Tanzanian shillings.

r be the price of a shirt in Tanzanian shillings.

It implies that for the first option, the cost for 2 ties is Tshs 2p and the cost for 2 shirts is Tshs 2r.

Thus, the total cost for 2 ties and 2 shirts is 2p+2r.

For the second option, the cost for 1 tie is Tshs p and the cost for 3 shirts is Tshs 3r.

Therefore, the total cost for 1 tie and 3 shirts is p+3r.

From this information, form a system of linear simultaneous equations as follows:

{83000−(2p+2r)=900083000−(p+3r)=0

Simplifying simultaneous equations gives,

{p+r=37000(1)p+3r=83000(2)

Use substitution method to solve equations (1) and (2) as follows.

From equation (2), expressing p in terms of r gives

p=83000−3r(3)

Substitute equation (3) into equation (1), that is,

83000−3r+r=3700083000−2r=37000−2r=−46000r=−46000−2=23000

Hence, r=23,000.

Substitute r=23,000 into equation (3) to obtain the value of p. That is

p=83000−(3×23000)=83000−69000=14000

Therefore, the price of each tie is 14,000 Tanzanian shillings and the price of each shirt is 23,000 Tanzanian shillings.
