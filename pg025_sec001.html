Example 2.9

A wire is divided into two pieces of lengths 213 cm and 212 cm. Find the total length of the wire in decimals.

Solution

Given the lengths of the two pieces of wire as 213 cm and 212 cm. Add the mixed fractions as follows:

213+212=73+52
=296

Divide 29 by 6 using the long division method to obtain 4.83˙.

Therefore, the total length of the wire is 4.83˙ cm.
