Mathematics for Secondary Schools Student’s Book Form One

12.

The ages of a father and his son are in the ratio of 4 : 2, respectively.

How old is the father if the son is 20 years old?

13.

The ratio of the heights in centimetres of two goats is 8 : 18.

What is the height of the smaller goat if the larger goat is 45 cm tall?

14.

The scale on a map is 1:30,000.

What actual distance does a length of 12 cm on a map represent if a scale is given as a ratio of the distance on the map to the corresponding actual distance on the ground?

15.

A Tanzanian map was drawn on a scale of 1:10,000,000.

If the distance between Mbeya and Dodoma is approximately 589 km, how far apart in centimetres are the two regions on the map?

16.

A piece of carpet is 28 m long.

A shopkeeper divided it into three pieces in the ratio 1 : 2 : 5.

Find the length of the smallest and largest piece of carpet.

17.

At a football match, the ratio of children to adults watching the match is 1 : 5.

There are 80 children in the stadium and each adult ticket cost Tshs 10,000 and that of children is quarter of the adult’s ticket.

Find the total amount of money obtained from ticket sales.

18.

An athlete runs 200 metres in 50 seconds.

If he runs constantly, how much time will he use to run 1,000 metres?

19.

Juma read 81 pages of a book containing 144 pages.

Find:

(a) the ratio of a number of pages remaining to the pages read in its simplest form.

(b) the percentage of pages read.

20.

Express the ratio of 18 hours to 2 days in its lowest terms.

21.

Two consecutive counting numbers are in the ratio 2 : 7.

Find the numbers.

22.

Find (5x−2y):(3x−y) in its simplest form if x:y=4:9.

23.

The denominator of a fraction exceeds the numerator by 5.

If 4 is subtracted from the numerator and the denominator remains unchanged, the resulting fraction is 25.

Find the original fraction.

24.

In an isosceles triangle, the ratio of each of the equal sides to the third side is 3 : 1.

The perimeter of the triangle is 49 cm, find the length of each side.

25.

Six copies of Mathematics textbooks cost Tshs 66,000.

Find the cost of 8 copies of the same textbooks.
