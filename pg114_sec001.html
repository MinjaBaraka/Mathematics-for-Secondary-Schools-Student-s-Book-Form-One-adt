Solution

Given T=30+25(x−3). It implies that T≥155∘C is the inequality required to obtain the depth. That is, 30+25(x−3)≥155.

Solve the inequality to obtain the values of x. Thus,

25(x−3)≥155−30
25(x−3)≥125
x−3≥5
x≥8

Therefore, the minimum depth is 8 metres.
