Example 6.32

Solve graphically the following system of simultaneous equations.

x+y=1 and x−3y=3

Solution

The x and y-intercepts for x+y=1 are (1,0) and (0,1), respectively. Also, x and y-intercepts for x−3y=3 are (3,0) and (0,−1), respectively. Use the intercepts to draw the graphs as shown in the following figure.

The two lines intersect at the point S(112,−12). Therefore, x=112 and y=−12 is the solution of the given system of linear simultaneous equations.

Example 6.33

The average of two numbers x and y is 7 and three times y subtracted from x is 18. Find the numbers graphically.

Solution

Let x and y be the numbers. It follows that, the average of two numbers is x+y2=7 and three times when y is substracted from x is 3(x−y)=18.
