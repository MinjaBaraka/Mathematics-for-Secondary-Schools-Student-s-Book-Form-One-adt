Example 6.13

Find the equation of a straight line joining the points A (1, 6) and B (5, 9). Write the answer in form of ax+by+c=0.

Solution

Let (x1,y1)=(1,6) and (x2,y2)=(5,9).

Using y−y1x−x1=y2−y1x2−x1 it follows that,

y−6x−1=9−65−1
y−6x−1=34

Cross multiplication gives, 4y−24=3x−3.

Rearranging gives, 3x−4y+21=0.

Therefore, the equation of the line is 3x−4y+21=0.

Example 6.14

Find the equation of a line passing through the points (4,7) and (−2,−5), giving your answer in the form ax+by=c.

Solution

Let (x1,y1)=(4,7) and (x2,y2)=(−2,−5).

From y−y1x−x1=y2−y1x2−x1 it follows that,

y−7x−4=−5−7−2−4
y−7x−4=2

Cross multiplication gives,

y−7=2x−8
2x−y=1

Therefore, the equation of the line is 2x−y=1.
