Word problems involving inequalities

In real-life activities, there are some situations which require setting limits of quantities. Some of these activities include describing maximum number of people to attend a ceremony, minimum pass marks, maximum amount of salt required and so on. Such relationships can be represented and solved by using concepts of inequalities.
