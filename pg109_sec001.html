Example 5.41

Solve for the value of x if 5x+2>1.

Solution

Given 5x+2>1. Subtract 2 from both sides of the inequality. That is,

5x + 2 - 2 > 1 - 2
5x > -1

Divide by 5 on both sides

5x5>−15
x>−15

Therefore, x>−15.

Example 5.42

Find the value of x which satisfy the inequality −4x+3≥12.

Solution

Given −4x+3≥12. Subtracting 3 from both sides of the inequality to obtain,

−4x+3−3≥12−3
−4x≥−52

Divide by −4 on both sides of the inequality to get,

x≤58

Therefore, x≤58.

Example 5.43

Solve the inequality

5(x+2)−3x≤4+2x+3(x−1).

Solution

Given

5(x+2)−3x≤4+2x+3(x−1).

Opening brackets and simplifying gives,

2x+10≤5x+1

Collecting like terms gives,

2x−5x≤1−10
−3x≤−9

Divide by −3 on both sides of the inequality to get, x≥3. Therefore, x≥3.

Example 5.44

Indicate the solution of |x|≤2 on a number line.

Solution

Given |x|≤2, it implies that ±x≤2. Thus, either +x≤2 or −x≤2.

x≤2 or x≥−2
