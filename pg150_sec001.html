Finding the equation of a straight line given the gradient and x or y-intercepts

Given the gradient and x or y-intercept, the equation of a line can also be obtained directly by substituting these values in the equation, y=mx+c.

Example 6.21

Find the equation of a straight line whose gradient is 32 and its y-intercept is (0,3).

Solution

Given the gradient m=32 and the y-intercept (0,3), then substitute (0,3).

From y=mx+c, substitute m=32 and c=3 in this equation to obtain

y=32x+3.

Therefore, the equation of the line is y=32x+3.
