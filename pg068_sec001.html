Example 4.1

Simplify 48:36.

Solution

Given 48:36

The given ratio can be written in fraction as 4836.

Dividing the numerator and denominator by 12 gives,

4836=43

Therefore, 48:36 in its simplified form is 4:3.

Example 4.2

Given a:b=1:2 and b:c=3:5, find a:b:c and a:c.

Solution

Given a:b=1:2 and b:c=3:5.

b is in both ratios, so make the two ratios equivalent by multiplying a:b=1:2 by 3 and b:c=3:5 by 2 as follows:

a:b=(1:2)×3
b:c=(3:5)×2
a:b=3:6
b:c=6:10

Therefore, a:b:c=3:6:10 and a:c=3:10.
