Solution

Given the equation x+y=1. Re-writing it in the form y=mx+c gives y=−x+1.

Substitute the given values from the given table of values.

When x=−2, then y=−(−2)+1=3.

When y=2, x=−1.

When x=0, y=1.

When x=2, y=−1.
