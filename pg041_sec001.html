Chapter summary

  1. A rational number is any number written in the form of ab, where both a and b are integers and b≠0.
  2. All terminating and repeating decimals are rational numbers.
  3. A decimal number which neither terminates nor repeats is an irrational number.
  4. An irrational number cannot be written in the form of ab, where a and b are integers and b≠0.
