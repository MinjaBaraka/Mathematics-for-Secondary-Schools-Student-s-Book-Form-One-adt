Solution

Let:

x be the number of materials of type A.

y be the number of materials of type B.

Total units of the materials available are 20.

Total units of materials of type A and B are 4x and 3y respectively. Therefore, the equation relating the two types of materials is 4x+3y=20.

Exercise 5.4

Formulate a linear equation in each of the following statements.

  1. 1. Five times a number gives twenty.

  2. 2. The difference between 123 and another number is 150.

  3. 3. The sum of 21 and another number is 125.

  4. 4. When a certain number is increased by 15, the result is 88.

  5. 5. When 99 is decreased by a certain number, the result is 63.

  6. 6. The product of 12 and another number is the same as two times the sum of 12 and the number.

  7. 7. A number is such that, when it is doubled and 8 is added to it, the result is the same as multiplying the number by 3, and subtracting 7.

  1. 8. When 36 is added to a certain number, the result is the same as multiplying the number by 5.

  2. 9. Janeth’s mother is five times as old as Janeth. After 8 years, her mother will be 4 times as old as Janeth.

  3. 10. When the sum of n and (n+3) numbers is multiplied by 5, the result is two times the difference between the larger number and the smaller number.

  4. 11. The sum of one-third of a number and two-fifth of another number is 1.

  5. 12. The difference between one-half of a number and one-third of another number is 2.

  6. 13. Four times the value of a quantity equals the sum of 2 and a quarter of the value of another quantity.

Solving linear equations with one unknown

Solving an equation with one unknown means finding the value of the unknown variable which satisfy the equation. It involves several steps of simplification to obtain the value of the variable which satisfies the given equation.
