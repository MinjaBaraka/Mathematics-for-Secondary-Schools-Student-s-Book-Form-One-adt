Also, (2n+m)×3=(2n+m)+(2n+m)+(2n+m)

=(2n+2n+2n)+(m+m+m)

=6n+3m.

Therefore, (2n+m)×3=6n+3m.

The multiplication sign between a number and a bracket is normally dropped but the meaning remains the same.

For example;

3×(2n+m)=3(2n+m)=6n+3m and 3×(2n−m)=3(2n−m)=6n−3m.

In general, a(b+c)=ab+ac.

When multiplying an algebraic expression by a number, each term must be multiplied by the number.

Similarly, when dividing an algebraic expression by a number, each term must be divided by the number.

Example 5.5

Multiply 5a+2b−3c by 4.

Solution

(5a+2b−3c)×4=4(5a+2b−3c)

=4×5a+4×2b+4×(−3c)

=20a+8b−12c.

Therefore, 5a+2b−3c multiplied by 4 gives 20a+8b−12c.

Example 5.6

Multiply 2x−3y by −2a.

Solution

(2x−3y)×(−2a)=(−2a)(2x−3y)

=(−2a)(2x)+(−2a)(−3y)

=−4ax+6ay.

Therefore, when 2x−3y is multiplied by −2a, the result is −4ax+6ay.
