Example 6.12

Verify that y−7=3(x−3) represents an equation of the straight line passing through the point (3,7), hence determine its gradient and y-intercept.

Solution

A line passes through a point if its coordinates satisfies the equation representing the line.

Thus, substitute the coordinates of (3,7) in the equation of the line.

That is,

7−7=3(3−3)
0=0

Therefore, the line with given equation passes through the point (3,7).

Write the equation in the form of y=mx+c, as follows:

y−7=3(x−3)
y=3x−2

Comparing with y=mx+c, m=3 and c=−2.

Therefore, the gradient of the line is 3 and y-intercept is (0,−2).
