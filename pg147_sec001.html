Equation of a straight line when given a gradient and a point

The equation of a straight line can be determined when given a gradient m and a point A(x1,y1) lying on the line. Choosing another point P(x,y) which lie on the line, it follows that, m=y−y1x−x1

Cross multiplication gives, y−y1=m(x−x1)

This is the equation of a line with gradient m and passes through the point A(x1,y1).

Example 6.17

A straight line has a gradient of 34 and passes through the point (−1,2). Find:

(a) Its equation in the form y=mx+c

(b) The x and y-intercepts.

Solution

(a) The equation of this line is y−y1=m(x−x1).

Given m=34 and the point (x1,y1)=(−1,2).

Substitute the values in the equation to get,

y−2=34(x−(−1))
4(y-2) = 3(x+1)
4y = 3x + 11
y=34x+114

Therefore, the equation of the straight line is y=34x+114.
