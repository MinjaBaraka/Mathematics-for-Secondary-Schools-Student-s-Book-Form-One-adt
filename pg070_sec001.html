Example 4.8

A clothing shop sells t-shirts which are in three colours: red, blue, and green. The colours are in the ratio of 3 to 4 to 5, respectively. If the shop has 20 blue t-shirts, how many t-shirts are there in the shop?

Solution

Let x be the number of red t-shirts and y be the number of green t-shirts. Writing the items in ratios gives,
green:blue=5:4=y:20 and red:blue=3:4=x:20
Writing in fraction form gives,
greenblue=54=y20(i)
redblue=34=x20(ii)
From equation (i), it follows that,
54=y20
Cross multiplication gives,
4y=5×20
y=5×204
= 25
From equation (ii), it follows that,
34=x20
Cross multiplication gives,
4x=3×20
x=3×204
= 15
Thus, the total number of t-shirts is 15 + 20 + 25 = 60.
Therefore, the clothing shop has 60 t-shirts.
