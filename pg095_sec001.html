The son’s age after 4 years will be (x+4) years. The father’s age after 4 years will be (x+32+4) years =(x+36) years. After 4 years, the father’s age will be twice the age of his son. That is,

x + 36 = 2(x + 4)
x + 36 = 2x + 8
2x - x = 36 - 8
x = 28

The father’s present age =x+32

= 28 + 32
= 60

Therefore, the son’s present age is 28 years and the father’s present age is 60 years.
