Example 6.11

A straight line passes through the points P(−4,5) and Q(−2,1). Find its:

(a) Gradient
(b) Equation
(c) x and y-intercepts

Solution

(a) The gradient of the line is given by:

m=y2−y1x2−x1

Let (x1,y1)=(−4,5) and (x2,y2)=(−2,1). It implies that,

m=1−5−2−(−4)
=−42
=−2

Therefore, the gradient of a straight line is −2.

(b) Let A(x,y) be any point on the line. Take the points P(−4, 5) and A(x,y) to determine the equation of the line, that is,

m=y−5x−(−4)
But m=−2.

Thus,

−2=y−5x+4
−21=y−5x+4

Cross multiplication gives,

(y−5)=−2(x+4)
y−5=−2x−8
y=−2x−8+5
y=−2x−3

Therefore, the equation of the straight line is y=−2x−3.
