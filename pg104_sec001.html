Solving word problems on linear simultaneous equations

Simultaneous equations are useful in solving real-life problems involving more than one unknown which can be solved simultaneously. Such problems can be formulated from different real-life scenarios such as the routes of the journey, managing multiple loans, recipe proportions and many others. Formulating and solving word problems on simultaneous equations involve the following steps:

1. Let the unknowns be represented by variables.

2. Write the two simultaneous equations according to the requirements of the problem.
