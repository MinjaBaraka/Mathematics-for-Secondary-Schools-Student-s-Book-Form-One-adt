Equation of a straight line

Suppose that two points P(x1,y1) and Q(x2,y2) lie on a straight line, then the relationship between x and y coordinates of a point N(x,y) lying on PQ can be determined. If x1≠x2 then point N will join PQ to form a straight line only if the gradient of line PN is the same as the gradient of line PQ as shown in Figure 6.5.

The gradient of PQ=

y2−y1x2−x1=m(1)

The gradient of PN=

y−y1x−x1=m(2)

Equations (1) and (2) gives the same results. From equation (2), it implies that y−y1=m(x−x1). Making y the subject gives y=m(x−x1)+y1. Further simplification gives y=mx−mx1+y1. Rearrangement gives y=mx+(y1−mx1). Let c=y1−mx1, it follows that y=mx+(y1−mx1) is simplified to

y=mx+c(3)
