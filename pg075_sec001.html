Chapter summary

  1. A ratio is a comparison between two or more quantities which are in the same unit and can be simplified as a fraction.
  2. A ratio can be expressed as a fraction, using colon or in words.
  3. A proportion is a statement that two ratios are equal.
  4. In general, a proportion describes a part, a share or a number considered in comparison to a whole.
