Solving word problems involving linear equations

Solving word problems on linear equations involve formulating the corresponding equations. The formulation of an equation requires careful interpretation of the given word problem. Once the equation has been formulated, the usual procedures for solving the equation are used.

Example 5.26

If John has 5,600 Tanzanian shillings, how many oranges can he buy if each orange costs 200 Tanzanian shillings?

Solution

Let x be the number of oranges that John can buy. The cost of x oranges is Tshs 200x. Thus, 200x=5600

x=5600200
= 28.

Therefore, John can buy 28 oranges.

Example 5.27

The age of a father is four times the age of his son. If the sum of their ages is 50 years, find the age of the son.

Solution

Let x be the age of the son. Thus, the age of the father is 4x years. It follows that,

x + 4x = 50
5x = 50
x=505
= 10

Therefore, the age of the son is 10 years.

Example 5.28

The sum of two consecutive integers is 31. Find the smaller number.

Solution

Since the two numbers are consecutive, then if the first integer is n, the second integer is n+1

Thus, n+(n+1)=31

2n + 1 = 31
2n = 30
22n=302
n = 15

Therefore, the smaller number is 15.

Example 5.29

A father is 32 years older than his son. After 4 years, the father’s age will be twice the age of his son. Find their present ages.

Solution

Let x be the son’s present age. Thus, the father’s present age is (x+32) years.
