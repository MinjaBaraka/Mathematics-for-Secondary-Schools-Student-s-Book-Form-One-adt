Example 6.15

A straight line is given by an equation 4x+y+6=0. Find:

  • (a) Gradient
  • (b) x and y-intercepts

Solution

Given 4x+y+6=0. Express the equation in the form y=mx+c. That is, y=−4x−6. Thus,

(a) gradient =−4

(b) c=−6 and therefore y-intercept is (0,−6).

From y=−4x−6, substitute y=0. It follows that,

0 = -4x - 6
4x = -6
x=−32

Therefore, x-intercept is (−32,0).

Example 6.16

Find the x and y-intercepts of the straight line 3x−2y+10=0.

Solution

Given 3x−2y+10=0. When x=0, it implies that 2y=10. Thus, y=5.

Therefore, the y-intercept is (0,5).

The x-intercept is obtained when y=0. It implies that 3x=−10, thus, x=−103.

Therefore, x-intercept is (−103,0).
