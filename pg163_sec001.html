Example 6.31

Find graphically the solution of the following pair of linear simultaneous equations.

3x−y=2 and 2x−y=3.

Solution

Given the two equations 3x−y=2 and 2x−y=3. Re-write the equation in the form y=mx+c and construct a table of values of each equation as follows:

Table of values for y=3x−2

x −2 −1 0 1 2
y −8 −5 −2 1 4

Table of values for y=2x−3

x −2 −1 0 1 2
y −7 −5 −3 −1 1

Locate the ordered pairs of values for y=3x−2 and y=2x−3 on the same xy-plane and draw the graph of each linear equation and identify the point of intersection of the two lines. The following is the graph representing the two equations.

From the graph, the two lines intersect at a point (−1,−5).

Therefore, x=−1 and y=−5 is the solution of the system of linear simultaneous equations.
