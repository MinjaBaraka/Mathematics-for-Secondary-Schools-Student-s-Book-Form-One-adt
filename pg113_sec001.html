Example 5.47

If 5 is subtracted from twice a certain number, the result is less than 11. Find the number.

Solution

Let y be the required number. The corresponding inequality is

2y-5<11.

Adding 5 on both sides gives

2y-5+5<11+5
2y<16

Divide by 2 on both sides of the equation to get

2y2<162
y<8

Therefore, y<8.
