Example 6.3

Find the gradient of the line joining points C (2, 7) and D (6, 1).

Solution

Given the points C (2, 7) and D (6, 1). Let (x1,y1)=(6,1) and (x2,y2)=(2,7).

From m=y2−y1x2−x1, it follows that

m=7−12−6
=6−4
=−32

Therefore, the gradient of the line joining the points C and D is −32.
