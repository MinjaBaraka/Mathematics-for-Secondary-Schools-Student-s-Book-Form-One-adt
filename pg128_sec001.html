Example 6.3

Find the gradient of the line joining points C (2, 7) and D (6, 1).

Solution

Given the points C (2, 7) and D (6, 1). Let (x₁, y₁) = (6, 1) and (x₂, y₂) = (2, 7). Let (x1,y1)=(6,1) and (x2,y2)=(2,7).

From m = y₂ − y₁ x₂ − x₁ , it follows that From m=y2−y1x2−x1, it follows that

m = 7−1 2−6
m=7−12−6
= 6 −4
=6−4
= − 3 2
=−32

Therefore, the gradient of the line joining the points C and D is − 3 2 . Therefore, the gradient of the line joining the points C and D is −32.
