Example 5.11

Express each of the following algebraic expressions as a single fraction:

(a) h−13+h+12

(b) b−2a4b−b+3a20b

Solution

(a) h−13+h+12 (The LCM of 3 and 2 is 6)

=2(h−1)+3(h+1)6 (Open the brackets)

=2h−2+3h+36 (Collect the like terms)

=5h+16.

Therefore, h−13+h+12=5h+16.

(b) b−2a4b−b+3a20b

=5b−10a−b−3a20b (Collect the like terms)

=4b−13a20b.

Therefore, b−2a4b−b+3a20b=4b−13a20b.
