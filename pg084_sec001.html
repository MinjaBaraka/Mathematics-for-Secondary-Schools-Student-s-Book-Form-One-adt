Algebraic expressions involving fractions and brackets

When dealing with algebraic expressions involving fractions and brackets, the brackets are opened by multiplying each term inside the brackets by the fraction, and simplifying the expression.

Example 5.9

Simplify the following algebraic expressions:

(a)14(8b+12b−b)
(b)15(15g+5g)

Solution

(a)14(8b+12b−b)=8b4+12b4−b4
=8b+12b−b4
=19b4.
Therefore, 14(8b+12b−b)=19b4.
(b)15(15g+5g)=15g5+5g5
= 3g + g
= 4g.
or 15(15g+5g)=15g+5g5
=20g5
= 4g.
Therefore, 15(15g+5g)=4g.
