From Figure 6.2, the gradient m of the straight line AB is determined by using the following formula:

m=change in y (vertical increase)change in x (horizontal increase)
Thus, m=y2−y1x2−x1=y1−y2x1−x2.

The following are the procedures for calculating the gradient of a straight line joining two points A(x1,y1) and B(x2,y2).

  1. 1. Write down the formula for calculating the gradient, m=y2−y1x2−x1.
  2. 2. Assign values to (x1,y1) and (x2,y2).
  3. 3. Substitute the values into the formula to obtain the gradient m.
  4. 4. Write the final answer which is the gradient m.

Note:

When finding the gradient, the order of finding the difference between the coordinate values in the formula does not matter. One may choose to find the difference from the second point or may choose to find the difference from the first point provided that the coordinates are subtracted correspondingly.
