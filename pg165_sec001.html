Thus, the two equations are x+y=14 and x−y=6. The x and y-intercepts are (14, 0) and (0, 14) for x+y=14 and (6, 0) and (0,−6) for x−y=6. Use the intercepts to draw the graph as shown in the following figure.

From the figure, the two lines intersect at a point (10,4). That is, x=10 and y=4. Therefore, the numbers are 4 and 10.
