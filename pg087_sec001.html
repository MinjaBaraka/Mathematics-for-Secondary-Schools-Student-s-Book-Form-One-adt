Example 5.12

The sum of two numbers is 20. If one of the numbers is 8, form a linear equation connecting the two numbers.

Solution

Let x be the unknown number. Since the known number is 8, then the algebraic equation connecting the two numbers is 8 + x = 20.

Example 5.13

The difference between 24 and another number is 8. Form a linear equation connecting the numbers.

Solution

Let y be the unknown number. Since the known number is 24, then the algebraic equation connecting the two numbers is either 24 - y = 8.

Example 5.14

The difference between two numbers is 48. Form a linear equation which describes the relationship between the two numbers.

Solution

Let z be one of the numbers and p be the other number. Since the difference between the two numbers is 48, and it is not specified which number is larger or smaller than the other, then the equations which relates the numbers is either z - p = 48 or p - z = 48.
