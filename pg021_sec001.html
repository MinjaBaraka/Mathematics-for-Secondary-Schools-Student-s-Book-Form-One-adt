Example 2.6

Convert each of the following decimals into fractions.

(a) 9.2˙
(b) 5.2˙3˙
(c) 2.105˙

Solution

(a) 9.2˙
Let x=9.2˙
(i)

Multiply by 10 both sides of equation (i) to get,

10x = 92.\dot{2}
(ii)

Subtract equation (i) from equation (ii) and solve for x as follows:

10x - x = 92.\dot{2} - 9.\dot{2}
9x = 83
x=839
Therefore, 9.2˙ into fraction is 839.
